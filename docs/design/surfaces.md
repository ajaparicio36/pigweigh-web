## Anonymous estimation

Mode: **Operate**. The visitor's job in this experimental thesis prototype is to provide handheld top/side phone photos of the same pig, resolve capture problems, and understand a defensible result. Content must distinguish top and side, show photo review/retake, explain supported capture bounds once calibrated, and distinguish upload, validation, inference unavailable, rejected capture, and success. A valid result needs units and valuation provenance; confidence must not imply weight certainty. Thesis context must remain clear without inventing accuracy or trading-fitness claims.

## Admin price publication

Mode: **Operate**. The admin signs in, reviews applicable price information, and publishes a positive price with reference/effectivity information. Show validation and commit outcome clearly. Session verification and publication authorization belong to server checks.
