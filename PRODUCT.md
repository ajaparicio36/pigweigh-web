## Users and purpose

This is an experimental research thesis project. Anonymous visitors provide handheld phone top/side photos of the same pig for estimated live weight and Philippine peso valuation. Admins publish the applicable price per kilogram. Controlled-pen capture uses flat ArUco cards attached near measured back/flank regions: `DICT_4X4_50`, ID 0, black-square side 100 mm (10 cm), excluding the white margin. The implemented [CPU bundle method](docs/inference.md) runs pose models, per-view homography, named length/width/depth proxies, Ramanujan II ellipse girth and the approved baseline `W0_kg=G_cm²*L_cm/14451`. Formula output precedes scale-error evaluation; regression is optional refinement. Attached references do not make the curved body coplanar, and configured 100 mm is not proof of the physical print scale. Accuracy and trading fitness remain unmeasured.

## Required workflows

- Require both photos for each estimation request; support upload or an explicit **Take photo** camera modal, then review, replace or remove before submission. Manual whole-pig and back/flank marker guides assist placement; they do not detect or validate captures and are excluded from saved photos.
- Automatically upload each successfully selected or captured photo for a server check, one at a time. Show checking, passed, rejected or retryable status beside its review; require both current photos to pass before estimating. Replacing/removing a photo invalidates its check, including same-name replacements. Live camera video stays local; selected photos are not retained. Final submission uploads and checks both photos again.
- Add a **Print marker** page at `/marker` to the main navigation, with a fixed A4 PDF for `DICT_4X4_50`, ID 0 and a 100 mm black square. Print at 100% / Actual size with Fit to page disabled, then verify the black edges with a ruler. This is a compatible fixed asset, not an arbitrary marker generator; see [marker assets](docs/marker-assets.md).
- Return a clearly experimental formula estimate when compatible pose models, explicit marker/geometry configuration and the implemented pipeline produce usable measurements. Otherwise explain unavailable capability or rejected capture. Prior regression fitting or accuracy evaluation is not required for the selected prototype.
- Show the price per kilogram and its provenance alongside a valuation. If no usable price exists, preserve a valid weight result and explain that valuation is unavailable.
- Verify admin sessions on the server and restrict price publication to authenticated admins.
- Preserve the original PigWeigh cream/red colors in the implemented anonymous and admin interfaces.
