# Frontend direction contract

Mode: Operate, for anonymous estimation and admin price publication. Implemented from the user brief and lead's code-led direction; no approved visual comp or user-selected concept is claimed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the paired capture and price provenance legible before any numerical result. Every outcome reflects the real service.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve cream, dark red and primary red. System sans, native forms, 12px bordered panels and 8px controls; no institutional logos or inherited composite backgrounds.

STORY: The visitor reviews the current price, supplies top and side views of the same pig, and submits with visible readiness. The admin verifies sign-in and publishes a referenced price, reviewing conflicts before retrying.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact dark red navigation, left-aligned task title, current-price strip, then two equal photo work areas on desktop. Each work area has its view label, complete photo preview and native selection/replace control. Readiness and submission sit directly below. Phones stack both views; admin uses a focused sign-in form or current price and publication form.

FORM: Paired photo review is the signature interaction, with direct replacement and separate view labels. Lead-directed functional composition; no concept seed, comp or synthetic result.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Verification boundary: builder runs static tests and one detector scan. Browser QA and visual review pass to the default worker after resource confirmation. No shipping raster assets are added.
